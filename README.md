# Computer Room: Access to Tools

KISD, WS26 · Ben Drusinsky, Prof. Dr. Lasse Scherffig

> *"The WHOLE EARTH CATALOG functions as an evaluation and access device."*
> Whole Earth Catalog, Fall 1968

Smart home objects are designed for seamless comfort. They are black boxes that promise
convenience while hiding how they work and what they cost. This course questions "smart"
objects through deliberate acts of inconvenience and revelation, and treats documentation
as a design medium: a black box stays closed when nobody writes down how it works.

We start with computers you can read and change line by line, then look at the manuals,
kits and catalogues of the 1960s and 70s (Enzo Mari, Ken Isaacs, the *Whole Earth Catalog*),
and end with objects that ship with a manual someone else, human or agent, can follow.
